# Our Guinea

Current direction approved in Barry's detailed brief on 2026-09-27. This supersedes older one-step-at-a-time implementation instructions in GUINEA_APP_MASTER_BRIEF.md.

Our Guinea helps Guineans reconnect with language, family stories and cultural knowledge—wherever they live.

Primary users: diaspora adults, older teenagers, parents and people reconnecting with family language and heritage. Children may participate with adults; no child accounts.

Inclusive Guinea-wide identity. Guinean Pular is the first planned module because Barry can help source and verify it. Other languages and communities must fit later. Do not equate Guinea with one region or ethnicity.

Positioning: human-verified language, authentic speaker audio, English/French explanations and meaningful family conversations. Do not copy a maps/leaderboards/AI-guide product. The competitor description supplied by Barry is product context, not independently verified research.

This slice: polished Home → clearly labelled Pular-content placeholder → demonstration meaning choice and feedback → family mission → results → locally saved completion → Home. Preserve existing Family prompts and childhood shortcut.

Without reviewed content, this is a functional demonstration, not a Pular lesson. No invented words, synthetic pronunciation or false verification claims. A text cue makes the demonstration answerable while audio is unavailable. A full family conversation is intended to take 3–5 minutes; the UI alone does not enforce that duration.

No accounts, uploads, recordings, advertising, payments, social features, API keys or backend. Only interface language and prototype completion progress are persisted locally. Private memory preservation is a future direction, not part of this slice. Validate usefulness before expansion or monetisation.
